*A series of design-and-make studies from Lawrenceville's design studio — each
one a loop of concept sketch → Fusion 360 model → laser-cut/3D-printed
fabrication → finishing.*

## Makeup & Jewelry Organizer

A **client-briefed** build: I interviewed my client, wrote a problem-solving
brief, and iterated from a cardboard low-res mock-up before committing to wood.
The final organizer packs three drawers (one hidden), 3D-printed jewelry inserts,
a sliding secret compartment, and a hand-painted panel — all sized to still fit
inside her drawer.

## Parametric Pencil Box

A deceptively complex parametric box: two small storage compartments, a
pull-out drawer with an internal stopper, and a larger bay — plus engraved
detailing on the walls. I laid out all the walls first, then added the
finger-joints last, which made the tedious joinery far easier to reason about.
The final build stayed remarkably close to my concept sketch.

## 360° Phone Stand

A Fusion 360 phone stand built around adjustability: a hinged arm for tilt, a
hidden disk in the base that lets the whole stand **swivel 360°**, a slot that
holds an AirPods case, and cutouts under the charger, speaker, and microphone so
the phone stays usable — and audible — on calls while docked.

![The full phone-stand design — hinged arm, base, and headphone holder.](/images/cad-projects/phone-stand.png)

![The hidden rotating disk in the base that gives the stand its 360° swivel.](/images/cad-projects/phone-stand-swivel.png)

## Articulating LED Lamp

An articulating desk lamp with an upper and lower arm joined by circular metal
plates, with **magnets epoxied into the joints** so the arm can rotate and hold
its pose.
