*A series of design-and-make studies from Lawrenceville's design studio — each
one a loop of concept sketch → Fusion 360 model → laser-cut, 3D-printed or
hand-finished fabrication → finishing. Some are precise laser work; others lean
on pyrography, hand-painting and veneer.*

## Makeup & Jewelry Organizer

My most involved studio build, and a **client-briefed** one: I interviewed my
client, wrote a problem-solving brief, and iterated from a cardboard low-res
mock-up before committing to wood. The whole thing had to disappear back into
her existing dresser drawer, so every compartment is sized to that footprint.

The finished organizer is a small piece of clever packaging: **stepped, tiered
compartments** that let you see everything at once, **two hand-painted panels**
(a sunflower and a hibiscus) that slide in as the top cover, **3D-printed
dividers** I modeled to sort jewelry, a **flip-up lidded compartment**, and a
**tiny secret drawer** with a scalloped finger-pull. The whole body is wrapped
in a **pyrography meadow** — hand-burned wildflowers, bees and butterflies.

## Parametric Pencil Box

A deceptively complex parametric box: two small storage compartments, a
pull-out drawer with an **internal stopper** so it can't fall out, and a larger
bay — plus a laser-**engraved chevron pattern** on one end panel. I laid out all
the walls first and added the finger-joints last, which made the tedious joinery
far easier to reason about. The final build stayed remarkably close to my
concept sketch.

![The finished pencil box: laser-cut plywood with a chevron-engraved end panel, open bays, and a pull-out drawer with a tab handle.](/images/projects/cad-projects/pencil-box-finished.jpg)

## 360° Phone Stand

A Fusion 360 phone stand built around adjustability: a hinged arm for tilt, a
hidden disk in the base that lets the whole stand **swivel 360°**, a slot that
holds an AirPods case, and cutouts under the charger, speaker, and microphone so
the phone stays usable — and audible — on calls while docked.

## Articulating LED Lamp

An articulating desk lamp with an upper and lower arm joined by circular metal
plates, with **magnets epoxied into the joints** so the arm can rotate and hold
its pose.

![The finished LED lamp — a laser-cut plywood arm with rectangular cutouts, jointed to a stacked-ply round base with an on/off switch.](/images/projects/cad-projects/led-lamp.jpg)

## Wavy-Front Marquetry Box

A small **two-drawer box** with an undulating, sculpted front, built in
**walnut and maple** — the dark walnut body and top set against the pale maple
of the wavy drawer fronts and their matching curved pulls. It's the veneer-and-
solid-wood counterpart to the laser work above, and it was made in the same shop
as my [foldable marquetry lap desk](/projects/marquetry-desk/) — the two are
photographed together below.

![A wavy-fronted two-drawer box in walnut and maple, its curved maple drawer pulls echoing the undulating front, sitting on the makerspace bench beside the folding marquetry lap desk.](/images/projects/marquetry-desk/desk-03.jpg)
